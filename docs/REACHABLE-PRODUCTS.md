# What is reachable with the evidence that exists

Generated by `scripts/reachable_products.py` from the 100-roster corpus. `docs/SOURCE-HUNT.md` concluded that the pairing board's source requirement cannot be met on a permitted route. Rather than leave that as a dead end, this measures each alternative against the same corpus.

Corpus: 131 observations over 49 of 100 roster people, and 239 scorable relationship episodes.

| Option | Unit | Count | Gives up |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. The pairing board, as specified | pairings with both sides scored, same evidence shape | **1** of 239 | nothing |
| B. Pairing board, comparability caveat shown instead of enforced | pairings with both sides scored, any shapes | **5** of 239 | the claim that a gap is about the two people |
| C. Recognition timeline per person | people with two or more judged years | **24** of 100 | the pairing idea entirely; there is no WAR and no gap |
| D. Standing leaderboard, no pairings | people with two or more independent publishers | **13** of 100 | the pairing idea, and it inherits the shape confound directly into the ranking |
| E. One-sided pairing view | episodes where exactly one partner is judged | **147** of 239 | the signed gap, the mirroring, and the WAR metric |

## Reading each one

### A. The pairing board, as specified

Not reachable. One comparable pairing across 239 episodes.

### B. Pairing board, comparability caveat shown instead of enforced

Five rows. Four of them compare an award against a list placement, so most of the board would be measuring publication format.

### C. Recognition timeline per person

Reachable now. Shows when each person was recognised, by whom, with the evidence — and never compares two people.

### D. Standing leaderboard, no pairings

Reachable but thin, and it ranks people by how much coverage they got, which is closer to fame than to appearance.

### E. One-sided pairing view

Large, and it cannot produce a gap: a gap needs two estimates. It could show 'who was recognised while with whom', which is a different claim.

## The decision this leaves you

Option A is the product as specified and it is not reachable. Everything else trades away part of the original idea: B keeps the board and loses the claim that a gap is about two people; C and D keep the evidence and lose the pairing; E keeps the pairing and loses the gap.

*This recommends nothing. Each option trades away something different and which trade is acceptable is not an engineering decision.*
